How these connections are used

Practice checks supported answers against the supplied answer key. Thinking is checked only when a decision or inference model is configured. A speech connection adds transcription; a decision connection adds learning signals from original speech. With an inference connection, unresolved work receives a deeper review and optional hints.

Saving or opening this page does not start inference or use your microphone.
